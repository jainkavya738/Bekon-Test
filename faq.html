Frequently Asked Questions

Got questions? We've got answers. If you can't find what you're looking for, feel free to reach out to our team.

Do I need to know how to code to use NovaUI?

While having a basic understanding of HTML and CSS is helpful, our UI kit is designed to be as plug-and-play as possible. You can simply copy and paste our components directly into your project.

Can I use NovaUI for commercial projects?

Absolutely! Depending on the pricing plan you choose, you can use our components for an unlimited number of personal and commercial projects without any strict attribution requirements.

How often do you release updates?

We push minor updates and bug fixes weekly. Major feature updates, including new components and templates, are typically released on a monthly basis.

Do you offer refunds?

Yes, we offer a 14-day money-back guarantee. If you are not satisfied with NovaUI for any reason, contact our support team and we will issue a full refund, no questions asked.
