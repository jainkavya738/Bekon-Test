About Us

We are a dedicated team of designers and engineers committed to building the future of the web.

Our Mission

At NovaUI, our mission is to empower creators, developers, and businesses by providing them with the most intuitive, beautiful, and high-performance UI components available on the market. We believe that great design shouldn't be a luxury—it should be the standard.

Our Journey

Founded in 2026, NovaUI started as an open-source side project to help a small community of developers prototype applications faster. Since then, it has evolved into a comprehensive framework trusted by thousands of companies worldwide, ranging from fresh startups to Fortune 500 enterprises.

Our Core Values

  • Design Excellence: We obsess over every pixel and every micro-interaction.
  • Performance: We believe speed is a feature, and we build lightweight, lightning-fast tools.
  • Accessibility: Technology should be for everyone, and our components are built to be inclusive from the ground up.
  • Community: We are driven by the feedback and passion of our global community.
